SHELF spider-man | Spider-Man

VOLUME xm-o1 | X-Men: Divided We Stand Omnibus Vol. 1 | Carey, David, Kyle & Yost, Brubaker | 2008 | 38 issues
ABOUT What Messiah Complex left on the floor. The mansion is rubble, the team is formally disbanded, Xavier's body is missing and Cable has jumped into the future with the first mutant born in a decade. Four books pick the pieces up at once — Carey rebuilding Xavier inside his own fractured head, Peter David watching Mutant Town drain out, Kyle & Yost standing up the black-ops X-Force that Cyclops will not admit exists, and Brubaker walking everyone west. Closes with the X-Men housed in San Francisco, which is the status quo for the next three books.
